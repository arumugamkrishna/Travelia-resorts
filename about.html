Our Story

Travelia Resorts was born from a singular conviction: that modern luxury should not isolate you from nature, but connect you intimately with it. Founded in 2010 by visionary hospitality connoisseurs, our journey began with a single secluded coastal retreat in South Goa.

Over the past fifteen years, Travelia has evolved into an internationally revered collection of eco-conscious resort sanctuaries spanning the Maldives, Bali, the Swiss Alps, Dubai, and Paris. Each destination celebrates indigenous craftsmanship, timeless architecture, and genuine heartfelt service that treats every guest as royalty.

“We don't merely provide a bed and a view; we craft sanctuaries where souls unwind, stories unfold, and memories linger for a lifetime.”
